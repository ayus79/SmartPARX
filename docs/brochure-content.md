## 6. Safety

### Safety features
1. **Auto-lock on both columns with spring-return mechanism:** default on all models; stops the platform from falling if the hydraulics fail or leak.
2. **Height-adjustable limit switch:** prevents motor overload and platform overtravel; lets the platform height be adjusted to the slab/beam bottom.
3. **Car presence sensor + height sensor:** the presence sensor stops the upper car lowering onto a car below; the height sensor stops the car touching the slab/beam/services.

### Key rules & regulations (user guidance)
- Keep speed **5–10 km/h** while parking on the system
- **Do not wash cars** on or below the platform
- The car **must touch the stop bar** of the platform
- Approach slope: max **4%** descending/ascending; incorrect approach angles cause problems SmartPARX can't be held liable for
- **Do not keep system anchors submerged in water**; this affects structural integrity
